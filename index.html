القوانين والخصوصية

نشكركم على استخدام منصة Madain Medical Association. تم إنشاء هذه المنصة لخدمة الطلاب وتسهيل الوصول إلى المصادر والمواد التعليمية الطبية.

نتقدم بخالص الشكر والتقدير لكل من ساهم في إنشاء وتطوير هذه المنصة، ونسأل الله أن يجزيهم خير الجزاء وأن يجعل هذا العمل نافعاً للطلاب والعلم.

الخصوصية وحماية البيانات: نحن نحرص على حماية بيانات المستخدمين وعدم استخدامها بشكل غير قانوني أو مشاركتها مع أي جهة دون سبب مشروع.

المحتوى الموجود في المنصة مخصص للأغراض التعليمية فقط، ولا يُعتبر بديلاً عن استشارة الطبيب أو المصادر الطبية المعتمدة.

يُمنع استخدام المنصة في أي نشاط غير قانوني أو محاولة الوصول غير المصرح به إلى بيانات أو أنظمة المنصة.

باستخدامك لهذه المنصة، فإنك توافق على الالتزام بهذه القوانين والسياسات واحترام حقوق أصحاب المحتوى.

🫁 Respiratory System — MCQs

Semester 2 — Dr. Osman Ahmed Osman

1. The physiological dead space:
  • A) Is exactly equal to the anatomic dead space in normal people
  • B) Increases during exercise
  • C) Is measured by single breath nitrogen analysis
  • D) Is higher in elderly than young adults
  • E) Is the same as the physiological shunt
Answer: D) Is higher in elderly than young adults
2. A high alveolo-arterial PO₂ difference most likely results from:
  • A) Low fractional concentration of O₂ in the inspired air
  • B) Hypoventilation
  • C) Arteriovenous shunt
  • D) Pulmonary edema
  • E) Polycythemia
Answer: D) Pulmonary edema
3. At 33 feet under water the ambient pressure is:
  • A) Two atmospheres
  • B) One and a half atmosphere
  • C) 33 pounds per square inch
  • D) Same as the pressure in a tunnel 33 feet below sea level
  • E) Half the pressure 33 feet above sea level
Answer: A) Two atmospheres
4. Which of the following combinations are typical findings in compensated respiratory alkalosis?
  • A) Low PaCO₂, low bicarbonate level and normal pH
  • B) Low PaCO₂, normal bicarbonate level and high pH
  • C) Low PaCO₂, low bicarbonate level and high pH
  • D) Low PaCO₂, normal bicarbonate level and normal pH
  • E) Low PaCO₂, high bicarbonate level and high pH
Answer: A) Low PaCO₂, low bicarbonate level and normal pH
5. The partial pressure of oxygen in the alveoli is expected to be least affected by:
  • A) Fractional concentration of O₂ in inspired air
  • B) Respiratory quotient
  • C) PCO₂ in alveoli
  • D) Hemoglobin concentration
  • E) Barometric pressure
Answer: D) Hemoglobin concentration
6. A veno-arterial shunt is likely to:
  • A) Decrease the cardiac output
  • B) Increase the pulse pressure
  • C) Increase venous return
  • D) Decrease PaO₂
  • E) Increase the peripheral vascular resistance
Answer: D) Decrease PaO₂
7. The arterio-venous difference of O₂ is highest across:
  • A) Liver
  • B) Kidney
  • C) Cardiac muscle
  • D) Skin
  • E) Brain
Answer: C) Cardiac muscle
8. In a patient, hypoventilation is most likely if:
  • A) PaO₂ is low
  • B) pH of arterial blood is low
  • C) PaCO₂ is high
  • D) O₂ in arterial blood is low
  • E) PCO₂ of expired air is high
Answer: C) PaCO₂ is high
9. A decrease in the recoil force of the lung is likely to:
  • A) Decrease total lung capacity
  • B) Increase vital capacity
  • C) Increase functional residual capacity
  • D) Decrease the residual volume
  • E) Decrease compliance of the lung
Answer: C) Increase functional residual capacity
10. Central chemoreceptors differ from chemoreceptors in that they:
  • A) Respond to changes in PaCO₂ indirectly through changes in CSF pH
  • B) Respond primarily to arterial O₂
  • C) Are located in the carotid bodies
  • D) Are insensitive to changes in hydrogen ion concentration
  • E) Are stimulated mainly by low arterial PO₂
Answer: A) Respond to changes in PaCO₂ indirectly through changes in CSF pH
11. At the end of maximal expiration the volume of air remaining in the lungs is:
  • A) FRC
  • B) RV
  • C) IRV + RV
  • D) TLC
  • E) VC
Answer: B) RV
12. At the end of expiration at sea level the PO₂ in the anatomic dead space is approximately:
  • A) 150 mmHg
  • B) 100 mmHg
  • C) 160 mmHg
  • D) 40 mmHg
  • E) 130 mmHg
Answer: A) 150 mmHg
13. Which is true regarding lung and chest wall mechanics?
  • A) At maximum expiration the lungs have no tendency to collapse but the chest has a great tendency to expand
  • B) At end of normal inspiration the recoil tendency of the lung is greater than that of the chest
  • C) During inspiration the recoil tendencies of both the lungs and chest increase
  • D) At the end of normal expiration the recoil tendency of the chest is slightly greater than that of the lung
  • E) The combined compliance is greater than the individual compliances of the lung and chest
Answer: B) At end of normal inspiration the recoil tendency of the lung is greater than that of the chest
14. Which is true concerning the diffusing capacity of the lungs?
  • A) It increases whenever the partial pressure gradient increases
  • B) It increases as the alveolar-capillary surface area rises
  • C) It increases as the thickness of the respiratory membrane rises
  • D) It is usually measured using carbon dioxide
  • E) It is usually measured using oxygen
Answer: B) It increases as the alveolar-capillary surface area rises
15. A person whose anatomic dead space is 100 mL breathes 12 times per minute with a tidal volume of 400 mL. His pulmonary ventilation is:
  • A) 1.2 liters/min
  • B) 2.4 liters/min
  • C) 3.6 liters/min
  • D) 4.8 liters/min
  • E) 6.0 liters/min
Answer: D) 4.8 liters/min
16. While repaying the O₂ debt:
  • A) The [H⁺] is increasing
  • B) PCO₂ is decreasing
  • C) Muscle blood flow is increasing
  • D) Respiratory rate is increasing
  • E) Minute volume is decreasing
Answer: C) Muscle blood flow is increasing
17. An average adult male has a vital capacity of:
  • A) 3 liters
  • B) 4 liters
  • C) 5 liters
  • D) 6 liters
  • E) 7 liters
Answer: B) 4 liters
18. The residual volume can be calculated by subtracting the expiratory reserve volume from:
  • A) Vital capacity
  • B) Inspiratory capacity
  • C) Functional residual capacity
  • D) Total lung capacity
  • E) Tidal volume
Answer: C) Functional residual capacity
19. The volume of air that describes the lung’s ability to eliminate carbon dioxide is:
  • A) Tidal volume
  • B) Pulmonary ventilation
  • C) Vital capacity
  • D) Alveolar ventilation
  • E) Functional residual capacity
Answer: D) Alveolar ventilation
20. Exchange of which of the following gases across the respiratory membrane is normally diffusion limited?
  • A) Oxygen
  • B) Carbon dioxide
  • C) Carbon monoxide
  • D) Nitrous oxide
  • E) Nitrogen
Answer: C) Carbon monoxide
21. Minute ventilation is:
  • A) The volume of air moved in or out of the alveoli per minute
  • B) The volume of air moved in or out of the lung per breath
  • C) Alveolar ventilation plus dead space ventilation
  • D) Invariably increases if the respiratory rate increases
  • E) Is measured using Bohr’s equation
Answer: C) Alveolar ventilation plus dead space ventilation
22. The ventilatory response to hypoxia:
  • A) Is independent of arterial PCO₂
  • B) Is mediated by the central chemoreceptors
  • C) Decreases gradually with time
  • D) Is largely mediated by the aortic bodies in humans
  • E) None of the above
Answer: C) Decreases gradually with time
23. With respect to oxygen and carbon dioxide transport in the blood:
  • A) For the same partial pressure a unit of blood carries the same amount of O₂ and CO₂
  • B) High PCO₂ favors O₂ binding to hemoglobin
  • C) Metabolic acidosis reduces PCO₂
  • D) Oxygenation of hemoglobin increases the affinity of hemoglobin to CO₂
  • E) Per unit volume of arterial blood there is more O₂ than CO₂
Answer: E) Per unit volume of arterial blood there is more O₂ than CO₂
24. PCO₂ in arterial blood:
  • A) Invariably increases with hypoxia
  • B) Stimulates ventilation mainly through peripheral chemoreceptors
  • C) Is the major controller of ventilation
  • D) Increases early in exercise
  • E) Increases on ascent to high altitude
Answer: C) Is the major controller of ventilation
25. In a normal subject, blood at the end of the pulmonary capillary:
  • A) Has a PO₂ similar to blood in the left ventricle
  • B) Has a PO₂ similar to that in the left atrium
  • C) Has a PO₂ higher than blood from the aorta
  • D) Has a percent saturation of 95
  • E) Contains 20 mL of CO₂ per 100 mL
Answer: A) Has a PO₂ similar to blood in the left ventricle
26. For a normal Hb-O₂ dissociation curve, the most correct relationship is:
  • A) PO₂ is 40 mmHg, percent saturation 50%
  • B) PO₂ is 95 mmHg, percent saturation 97%
  • C) PO₂ is 30 mmHg, percent saturation 50%
  • D) PO₂ is 60 mmHg, percent saturation 60%
  • E) PO₂ is 50 mmHg, percent saturation 50%
Answer: A) PO₂ is 40 mmHg, percent saturation 50%
27. Deficiency of surfactant may lead to:
  • A) Increased work of breathing
  • B) Increased compliance of the lung
  • C) Decreased recoil force of the lung
  • D) Bronchial asthma
  • E) An increase in functional residual capacity
Answer: A) Increased work of breathing
28. Continuous measurement of the concentration of nitrogen in the expired air can be used to measure:
  • A) Physiological dead space
  • B) Alveolar ventilation
  • C) Anatomic dead space
  • D) Functional residual capacity
  • E) Expiratory reserve volume
Answer: D) Functional residual capacity
29. Oxygen consumption of an average adult man per minute is about:
  • A) 250 ml
  • B) 350 ml
  • C) 400 ml
  • D) 500 ml
  • E) 550 ml
Answer: A) 250 ml
30. Which of the following is expected to increase functional residual capacity?
  • A) Surfactant deficiency
  • B) Lung fibrosis
  • C) Degeneration of lung elastic tissue
  • D) Pulmonary edema
  • E) Pulmonary congestion
Answer: C) Degeneration of lung elastic tissue
31. The most likely cause of hypoxic hypoxia together with hypercapnia is:
  • A) Ascent to a high altitude
  • B) Mild pulmonary edema
  • C) Veno-arterial shunt
  • D) Hypoventilation
  • E) Slight ventilation-perfusion mismatch
Answer: D) Hypoventilation
32. A decrease in the recoil force of the lung is expected to:
  • A) Increase vital capacity
  • B) Decrease residual volume
  • C) Decrease compliance of the lung
  • D) Increase functional residual capacity
  • E) Decrease total lung capacity
Answer: D) Increase functional residual capacity
33. Which of the following changes would shift the oxyhemoglobin dissociation curve to the right?
  • A) Decreased temperature
  • B) Decreased PCO₂
  • C) Increased pH
  • D) Increased 2,3-BPG
  • E) Decreased H⁺ concentration
Answer: D) Increased 2,3-BPG
34. The main stimulus for ventilation under normal resting conditions is:
  • A) Decreased arterial PO₂
  • B) Increased arterial PCO₂
  • C) Decreased arterial PCO₂
  • D) Increased arterial PO₂
  • E) Decreased hemoglobin concentration
Answer: B) Increased arterial PCO₂
35. The normal resting tidal volume in an average adult is approximately:
  • A) 100 mL
  • B) 250 mL
  • C) 500 mL
  • D) 1000 mL
  • E) 1500 mL
Answer: C) 500 mL
36. The normal anatomic dead space in an adult is approximately:
  • A) 50 mL
  • B) 100 mL
  • C) 150 mL
  • D) 300 mL
  • E) 500 mL
Answer: C) 150 mL
37. Which of the following conditions is most likely to decrease lung compliance?
  • A) Emphysema
  • B) Pulmonary fibrosis
  • C) Aging
  • D) Loss of elastic tissue
  • E) Increased surfactant activity
Answer: B) Pulmonary fibrosis
38. Which of the following is a major function of pulmonary surfactant?
  • A) Increase alveolar surface tension
  • B) Prevent alveolar collapse
  • C) Decrease lung compliance
  • D) Increase the work of breathing
  • E) Increase pulmonary vascular resistance
Answer: B) Prevent alveolar collapse
39. Which of the following best describes the ventilation-perfusion ratio in the normal lung?
  • A) It is exactly zero in all alveoli
  • B) It is approximately 0.8 overall
  • C) It is approximately 5 overall
  • D) It is independent of gravity
  • E) It is identical at the apex and base
Answer: B) It is approximately 0.8 overall
40. Which is true concerning the diffusing capacity of the lungs?
  • A) It increases whenever the partial pressure gradient increases
  • B) It increases as the alveolar-capillary surface area rises
  • C) It increases as the thickness of the respiratory membrane rises
  • D) It is usually measured using carbon dioxide
  • E) It is usually measured using oxygen

  • A) 1.2 liters/min

  • D) 4.8 liters/min
  • E) 6.0 liters/min

  • A) The [H⁺] is increasing

  • C) Muscle blood flow is increasing
